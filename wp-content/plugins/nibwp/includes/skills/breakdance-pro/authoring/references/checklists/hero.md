# Hero

- One `h1`, and it says what the page is — not a slogan that could belong to any site.
- Section → Container → content. Do not hang a heading off the root.
- Subheading earns its place or is cut. Two sentences of filler under a headline is the most common padding in generated pages.
- One primary action. A second competing button halves the first.
- Background image: set a real `alt`, or mark it decorative. Check text contrast over it.
- Spacing from the site's variables, not estimated pixels.
- Check it at the narrowest breakpoint. A hero that only works at 1440 is not finished.
